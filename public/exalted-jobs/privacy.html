Privacy policy

Exalted Jobs is a private, personal job-search application operated by Case Ollila. This policy describes the application, including its authorized Gmail connection. It does not replace the separate policies of Google, employers or other services the owner chooses to use.

Information accessed

When the owner authorizes a mailbox, Exalted Jobs can access its email address, Gmail message and thread identifiers, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, timestamps, labels, relevant message content and attachments needed for the application workflow. Searches may include historical application, recruiting and offer messages.

The Gmail permission requested is gmail.modify. It allows reading and managing email, including permitted draft, sending and labeling operations. The app uses these capabilities for the owner's job-search and account-verification workflow, subject to the owner's authorizations.

How information is used

  • Match messages to applications and employer accounts.
  • Track confirmations, interviews, rejections and offers, and check employer-contact history.
  • Process supported verification links or codes for authorized account workflows.
  • Maintain application evidence, status and owner notifications.

Unrelated messages are not imported wholesale into the application archive. Messages that cannot be correlated are retained with limited metadata, such as a hashed identifier and classification, for deduplication or owner review.

Storage and security

Application data is stored on the owner's PC and in owner-controlled backups. OAuth refresh credentials are encrypted in the application's credential vault. Stored application records and redacted email evidence are protected by the owner's device and application access controls; this is not a claim that every stored file or database field is individually encrypted.

Verification-message bodies are withheld from persistent email evidence to avoid archiving one-time links or codes. The public information pages do not expose inbox contents or application records.

Sharing and AI processing

Relevant candidate documents and information are sent to employers or their application-service providers when the owner authorizes those application actions. Gmail authorization and requests are handled by Google. This information website's hosting provider processes normal web requests.

The current Gmail processor uses rule-based classification. It does not send email bodies or verification codes to AI model providers. Separate candidate-document preparation can use the owner's configured AI provider. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models by Exalted Jobs.

Exalted Jobs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained for the owner's application tracking and evidence needs. Configurable retention cleanup applies to diagnostic and application-support artifacts; submitted evidence and backups may be retained separately. Revoking access does not automatically erase previously stored records or backups.

The owner can stop polling, revoke access through Google Account connections, and remove stored application data and backups using the application's administrative tools or the owner's device administration. For questions or deletion requests, contact casejobhunt@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
