Terms of use

Exalted Jobs is a personal job-application assistant operated by Case Ollila. It is intended for its owner's authorized job search and is not offered as a public application-submission service.

Authorized use

Use only accounts, mailboxes and candidate information that the owner is entitled to use. Application information must accurately describe the candidate. The owner determines job preferences, authorizes application actions and reviews declarations or agreements that require personal confirmation.

External services and human checks

Google, employers and application platforms provide their own services under their own terms. Their availability and requirements can change. Mandatory human verification, identity checks and unresolved submission results may require the owner's involvement. Exalted Jobs does not guarantee acceptance by an application platform.

Results and availability

The tool assists with preparation, tracking and authorized application actions. It does not guarantee an interview, offer, salary or employment outcome. The owner should keep suitable backups and check application evidence when reviewing results.

Privacy and access

The privacy policy describes Gmail access and data handling. The owner can stop using the tool or revoke Google access. This public website does not grant access to the private application workspace.
